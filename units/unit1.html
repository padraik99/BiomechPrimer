Unit 1: Muscle map, front and back

The order for this unit: 1 place the muscles blind, 2 decode the names, 3 study the model, then 4 retry when it opens in 2 days. That's 4 scenes and 22 spots. Plan on about 2 sessions plus the retry.

Place before you study. Drag a name onto a numbered spot, or tap a name and then tap the spot. Rate your confidence each time. Nothing is revealed now, and every name stays available on every spot, so the list never gives answers away by elimination. You will probably get most of these wrong, which is the point.

Muscle names are descriptions in Latin and Greek. Each one is named for its size, shape, location, number of heads, fiber direction or action (OpenStax 11.2). Decode the name and you have half the location for free.

Muscles work as a system. Learn them in families: three gluteals stacked by depth, four quadriceps on the front of the thigh (rectus femoris plus three vasti), three hamstrings behind, and adductors inside. In Unit 2 you'll see why each family pulls the way it does.

Readings: 11.6 pelvic girdle and lower limbs, 11.3 back, 11.4 quadratus lumborum.
